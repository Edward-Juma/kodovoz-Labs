MIRAI / 未来

Why Mirai Labs?

Tomorrow is something we build.

Mirai (未来) is the Japanese word for "future." We chose it because technology should do more than solve today's problems: it should create opportunities for tomorrow.

Labs represents curiosity, experimentation, and continuous improvement. We explore ideas, test solutions, refine experiences, and build products designed to evolve alongside the businesses they serve.

From concept to deployment, we bring strategy, design, engineering, and cloud technologies together to create products with lasting business value. We build for long-term success, not just launch day.

For us, the future isn't just a destination. It's something we engineer every day.

From idea to production

A clear path from first question to lasting product.

Each stage has a purpose, a shared checkpoint, and tangible outputs. The plan adapts to your product; the collaboration stays close.

  1. 01

    Align

    Discovery & consultation

    We learn your business, goals, audience, constraints, and project vision. Research and workshops help us define the right problem before shaping a solution.

    What takes shapeRequirements and user researchCompetitor analysisTechnical feasibility
  2. 02

    Plan

    Strategy & roadmap

    We turn goals into an actionable product direction, prioritizing the work that brings the clearest value.

    What takes shapeProduct roadmap and prioritiesUser stories and architectureTimeline and cost estimate
  3. 03

    Understand

    UX research & wireframing

    We map user journeys and validate functionality with low-fidelity layouts before visual design begins.

    What takes shapeUser flows and information architectureWireframesInteractive prototypes
  4. 04

    Explore

    UI design

    We create polished, responsive interfaces that express your brand and put usability and accessibility first.

    What takes shapeHigh-fidelity designsReusable design systemResponsive prototypes
  5. 05

    Build

    Engineering & development

    Our engineers turn validated designs into dependable software with modern technologies and maintainable architecture.

    Typical toolkitReact, Next.js, TypeScriptNode.js, Python, REST APIsDatabases, Docker, cloud services
  6. 06

    Prove

    Quality assurance & testing

    We test the product across devices and use cases, addressing quality before it reaches production.

    Quality checksFunctional and responsive testingAccessibility and performanceSecurity validation and fixes
  7. 07

    Prepare

    Deployment & production

    We prepare a secure production environment and verify the application in its real operating context.

    Release readinessBuilds, domains, SSL, and CDNDatabase and backup configurationMonitoring and production checks
  8. 08

    Operate

    Hosting & cloud infrastructure

    We match the hosting approach to your product's reliability, security, and growth requirements.

    Options may includeAWS, Azure, Google CloudVercel, Netlify, DigitalOceanCloudflare, Docker, managed data
  9. 09

    Launch

    Release & verification

    Launching is more than publishing code. We monitor the release and check that the product performs as expected.

    Launch checksPerformance and error monitoringAnalytics and SEO verificationFinal security review
  10. 10

    Grow

    Maintenance & continuous improvement

    Our partnership continues after deployment, keeping the product secure, useful, and ready for new demands.

    Ongoing careEnhancements, fixes, and updatesInfrastructure and analyticsSupport and product scaling
