Privacy Policy

Privacy and data protection

Kodovoz Labs is committed to protecting personal data and handling it responsibly in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2010 and related privacy principles. This notice explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you contact us, request a quote, or interact with our website.

This notice is designed to support lawful and transparent data handling and should be read alongside any project-specific agreement or consent form.

1. Information we collect

We may collect personal data such as:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, company name and project details submitted through a contact or quotation form.
  • Business information relevant to a service request or project discussion.
  • Website usage details such as IP address, browser information, device data, pages visited, and referral sources, where technically necessary for security, analytics or service stability.

2. How we use your information

We use personal data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries and project requests.
  • To assess and discuss service requirements.
  • To provide project updates, proposals and follow-up communication.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse and protect our systems.
  • To comply with legal, contractual and operational obligations.

3. Lawful basis and Kenya Data Protection Act, 2010

We process personal data in a manner consistent with the Kenyan Data Protection Act, 2010, including the principles of lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity, and confidentiality.

In practical terms, we rely on lawful grounds such as:

  • Your consent when you submit a form or request further information.
  • The need to perform a contract or take steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Our legitimate business interests, where balanced against your rights and interests.
  • Compliance with legal obligations or regulatory requirements.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only where necessary with:

  • Trusted service providers that support hosting, email delivery, analytics, customer support or security operations.
  • Professional advisers acting under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities where required by law or to protect our rights, property or safety.

5. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. These include secure hosting, access controls, and the minimisation of unnecessary personal data collection.

6. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, satisfy legal, accounting or operational requirements, or pursue a legitimate claim. When data is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymised where practical.

7. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Request deletion of personal data where appropriate.
  • Object to or restrict processing in certain situations.
  • Request transfer of your data where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on your consent.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.
